WELCOME TO CRICKET

More than just a game.

Cricket is played between two teams of eleven players. One team bats to score runs while the other bowls and fields to stop them. It brings people together through excitement, discipline and team spirit.

Batting

Batters score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets or sending it to the boundary.

Bowling

Bowlers aim to dismiss batters and limit the number of runs scored by the opposing team.

Fielding

Fielders work together to stop runs, catch the ball and support the bowler.

THREE POPULAR FORMATS

Different formats. Same spirit.

Cricket can be played in different formats, each with its own pace and strategy.

01

Test Cricket

Played over up to five days, with two innings per team. It tests patience, technique and endurance.

02

One Day International

Each team gets 50 overs. It combines long-term planning with exciting limited-overs action.

03

Twenty20 (T20)

Each team gets 20 overs. It is a fast-paced format full of quick decisions and big moments.

LEARN • PRACTISE • IMPROVE

Build your cricket skills.

Every great player develops through practice, patience and a willingness to learn. Good cricket is not only about talent — it is also about respect and teamwork.

  • Practise batting and bowling basics.
  • Improve catching and throwing.
  • Communicate clearly with teammates.
  • Respect teammates, opponents and umpires.
  • Enjoy the game and keep learning.
